# VinyLog — Product Context

## Product Purpose
A personal vinyl record collection tracker. Users log their records, browse the catalogue, and keep metadata (artist, year, genre, price, category). Built as a PHP school project but meant to feel like a real collector's tool.

## Users
Record collectors — mostly students and music enthusiasts who care about their physical collection. They use this on a laptop or desktop at home, browsing their collection in a relaxed evening mood. The aesthetic should feel warm, tactile, and a little retro — like the records themselves.

## Register
product

## Brand tone
Warm, confident, slightly nostalgic. Not corporate. Feels like a well-curated record shop, not a SaaS dashboard.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS cream (white cards, blue accents, Inter font everywhere)
- Dark hacker aesthetic
- Loud maximalism with too many colors
- Corporate sterility

## Strategic principles
- The collection is the hero — images of records should dominate
- Browsing should feel pleasurable, not utilitarian
- Actions (add, edit, delete) are secondary to discovery
